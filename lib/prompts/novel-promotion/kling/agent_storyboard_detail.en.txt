Character motion naturalness (added 2026-06-02):
In the [specific action] segment, don't just name the action — add these three points so Kling renders more naturally:
- Match manner-of-motion to the character: not just "walks forward" but "walks slowly with composed, dignified steps"; for stalking write "moves with light, cautious steps".
- Body coupling: when one part moves, others follow — "raises her head, gaze lifting with it, shoulders settling, hair swaying slightly".
- Break force-driven actions into physical stages (still 1 main action): for swinging a pole / throwing a punch / drawing a sword write its physical process as "wind-up, driving body part, main-action trajectory, settle/freeze" — these four are physical stages of the SAME action, comma-linked; do NOT use "→" arrows (→ marks separate action beats in this doc and would be miscounted as multi-action). e.g. "grips the pole and coils, weight dropping, then drives from waist and shoulders to sweep it in a wide arc from right to left, settling side-on with eyes fixed forward", not just "swings the pole".
- Multiple characters in frame: separate initiator from responder and spell out the interaction order (who acts → who responds → final relation), not just "two people facing each other".

Transformation / morph / shapeshift rule:
Generative models (Kling R2V / Seedance) CANNOT gradually morph one subject into a different
reference image in a single clip (e.g. "black cat lands and morphs into @Vera"). Reference
images are identity anchors; the model renders only one state, the morph is always lost and
following actions often dropped with it. When the source has "X morphs / transforms / takes
the form of / reveals true form as Y": split into TWO panels, express the change via the cut,
NEVER ask a single clip to fully replace the subject.
  Wrong (single-clip morph + multi-action): "black cat leaps, lands and morphs into @Vera,
    she walks to @William"
  Right (two panels): Panel 1 "a black cat leaps from the pillar shadow and lands lightly"
    (cat only); Panel 2 "@Vera now stands in place, steps toward @William" (human from the
    first frame, 1 main action)
Same for appear / vanish / summon / teleport — split into "before" + "after" panels.
